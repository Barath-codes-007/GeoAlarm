GeoAlarm

Never Miss Your Destination.

Set a location. Keep travelling. We'll remind you when you're getting close.

Download Android App Explore Features

Android 8.0 or newer. No account. Alarms and places stay on your phone.

Choose how early you want to know

Every alarm has a warning and an arrival. The warning fires once, then the alarm rings when you are inside your arrival radius (50, 100 or 200 m).

Built for the ride, not for attention

Everything you need to sleep on a train or bus and still get off at the right stop.

  • Location-based alarms

    Pick a place on the map. GeoAlarm rings when you arrive, even with the screen locked.

  • Custom alarm sounds

    Use your phone's ringtone, choose any audio file, or go with vibration only.

  • Interactive world map

    Pan, zoom, search or tap to choose a destination. Map data from OpenStreetMap.

  • Custom warning distance

    Get a heads-up at 500 m, 250 m, 100 m, on arrival, or at a distance you set.

  • Battery-aware monitoring

    Location updates slow down while you are far away and speed up as you get close.

  • Background monitoring

    A foreground service keeps watching with the app closed. Some phones need one extra setting.

  • Personal audio

    Your audio file stays on your phone. If it goes missing, GeoAlarm falls back to a default sound.

  • Alarm history

    See each trip: when you arrived, which warning you chose, and whether it finished or was cancelled.

  • Saved places

    Save Home, Work, a station or an airport, then set an alarm in two taps.

How it works

  1. Choose your destination

    Search for a place or tap the map.

  2. Select when to be warned

    500 m, 250 m, 100 m, at arrival, or your own distance.

  3. Start your GeoAlarm

    GeoAlarm begins watching your location in the background.

  4. Travel normally

    You get a warning first, then the alarm when you arrive.
