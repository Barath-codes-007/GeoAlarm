Privacy

Last updated: 21 September 2026. Applies to GeoAlarm v1.x as built from this repository.

Short version: GeoAlarm has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your location is used on your phone to measure the distance to your destination and is not sent to the GeoAlarm project. Only the map and place-search services listed below receive limited information when you use the map.

Why GeoAlarm needs location access

To know when you are approaching your destination. Location is read from your phone's location services only while you have an active alarm, or while you are choosing a destination on the map.

What stays on your device

  • Your alarms, saved places, recent destinations, history and settings, stored in the app's private database.
  • Your live location and the distance calculation. GeoAlarm keeps only the latest reading in memory while monitoring and does not record a location trail.
  • Your chosen alarm audio. GeoAlarm stores only a reference to the file and Android's permission to read it. The audio is never uploaded.

Android's cloud backup is switched off for GeoAlarm, so this data is not copied to your Google account by the app.

What leaves your device

Only when you use these features, and only to the services below. The project running this site does not operate them and does not receive this data.

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom (default build)
Showing the mapThe map tiles you view (their zoom and position) and your IP address, like any web request.The configured tile server. Default: OpenStreetMap's tile servers (OpenStreetMap Foundation).
Searching for a placeThe text you type, when you submit a search.The configured geocoder. Default: Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation).
Naming a tapped pointThe coordinates of the point you tap, to look up an address. You can turn this off in Settings; the point is then called “Dropped pin”.The same geocoder.
Location updatesNothing is sent by GeoAlarm. If Google Play services is present, GeoAlarm asks its fused location provider for readings, which Google handles under its own terms. Without it, GeoAlarm uses Android's built-in location manager.Google Play services (on your device) or Android.

People who build or distribute GeoAlarm can replace the tile and search providers. If you install a build from someone else, check which providers it uses. See docs/map-provider.md in the repository.

This website

The website is static. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and loads its fonts from its own files. If you choose a theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. If the site owner has connected the optional backend, your browser requests version information from it; the backend logs only the request path, status and timing, though the hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs such as IP addresses.

Deleting your data

  • Delete one alarm, place or history entry inside the app.
  • Settings → Data lets you clear history, clear recent destinations, or delete everything GeoAlarm has stored.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its database from your phone.

Children

GeoAlarm does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects none.

Changes and contact

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects privacy, this page and the repository's docs/privacy.md will be updated in the same release. Questions: see the contact page.
