Reporting a bug

Please include your phone model, Android version, GeoAlarm version, and what you expected to happen. Do not post your exact home or work coordinates in a public issue. If an alarm did not ring, say whether the phone was locked, whether the persistent notification was showing, and which battery setting the app had.
